## CRATE DIGGERS

Brainstorm

an interactive website that acts and a letterboxd for music. 

when you enter the webpage you'll be thrown into a record store where you can explore music enjoyed by other people on the website and friends. these songs will be binned in a sepearate crate like in a record store. 

you can also create a personalized page which will be a record player with a self on top displaying your favorite albums. you can play one of the songs on your record player (song you're currently enjoying). there'll be a couple of boxes underneath the record player that will lead to different lists/collections (by genre, by artist, favorites, want to listen too, etc.) 

you can rate the album entierly and individual songs as well. 
- potentially Beli rating system (swipe whether you like better or worse than some albums)
- or standard 5 star 

can also leave comments on the song/album
- like letterboxd can see other peoples comments and ratings
